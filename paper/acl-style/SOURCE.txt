acl.sty and acl_natbib.bst are the official *ACL style files, downloaded unmodified from
https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files (repository root) on 2026-09-13.

They are vendored here only so that `main.pdf` is reproducible from a clean clone with a
single command. The Overleaf ACL 2023 template ships its own copy (named acl2023.sty);
main.tex picks up whichever is present via \IfFileExists.
